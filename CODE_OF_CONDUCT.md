# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, evidence-minded community. Be considerate, critique ideas rather than people, disclose uncertainty, and give special care to language about children, families, disability, adversity, and mental health.

The following are unacceptable: harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, publishing another person's private information, uploading real case material, or presenting unvalidated software output as a diagnosis.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens people, privacy, or the project. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact route; do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue.

我们致力于建设尊重、包容、重视证据的协作社区。请批评观点而非个人，对涉及儿童、家庭、残障、困境和心理健康的表达保持克制与尊重。禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、公开他人隐私、上传真实个案，或把未经验证的软件结果描述为诊断。维护者可移除有害内容并限制参与；敏感问题请通过私密渠道报告。
